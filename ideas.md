# Crimson Education HK Dashboard — Crimson Atlas Visual Specification

## Ground-Truth Reference

The user-provided **Crimson Atlas screenshot dated 24 August 2026** is the visual ground truth for this revision. The HK dashboard should feel like a native area within the Crimson Atlas product rather than a separate editorial microsite. Fidelity to the reference takes priority over the previous dark editorial direction.

## Chosen Direction: Crimson Atlas Native Workspace

### Design Movement

The interface follows **contemporary enterprise knowledge-product design**: a bright, restrained application shell with compact navigation, light-grey rules, soft burgundy active states, and content presented as readable intelligence rather than marketing material.

### Core Principles

1. **Native product continuity:** the HK dashboard should look like it belongs inside Crimson Atlas.
2. **Information first:** typography, spacing, and component styling support scanning without competing with the content.
3. **Low visual noise:** white surfaces, subtle dividers, limited shadows, and restrained corner radii replace decorative treatment.
4. **Explicit status:** verified, source-backed, draft, and placeholder content remain easy to distinguish.

### Color Philosophy

White is the dominant workspace colour. Warm near-white and very light grey distinguish navigation, headers, table rows, and utility areas without creating heavy panels. Crimson burgundy is reserved for the brand mark, active navigation, labels, key figures, and primary emphasis. Draft content uses a pale amber treatment that remains quieter than active Crimson states.

### Layout Paradigm

The layout uses a **fixed white sidebar, slim top utility bar, contextual breadcrumb row, and a broad central workspace**. Tab navigation remains in the sidebar, while the main area starts with a compact title and section navigation rather than a large hero. Content blocks are contained in low-radius white panels with thin borders.

### Signature Elements

- A Crimson Atlas-inspired `C` mark and spaced uppercase wordmark treatment.
- Pale burgundy active navigation fills and thin crimson selection bars.
- Compact top utility controls, breadcrumb context, and small outlined status badges.

### Interaction Philosophy

Navigation and filtering should feel immediate. Hover states use small colour and border changes rather than elevation or scale. Disclosures expand within the reading flow. Search and filters remain visually compact and keyboard accessible.

### Animation

Use only short opacity and colour transitions between 120 and 180 milliseconds. Avoid cinematic entrances, large movement, glowing effects, and decorative parallax. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

Use **Source Sans 3** throughout the product shell, navigation, headings, tables, and body content. Hierarchy comes from weight, size, spacing, and case rather than a display-serif pairing. Page titles are approximately 26–32 px, section headings 20–24 px, module headings 14–18 px, and supporting copy 12–14 px.

### Brand Essence

**The HK market intelligence area inside Crimson Atlas: locally specific, immediately useful, and operationally clear.** Personality: precise, familiar, discreet.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are factual and short. Labels name the information type. Helper copy explains how to use a module or whether content is pending. Example: **“HK market overview”** and **“Compare curricula across systems.”**

### Wordmark & Logo

Use a compact Crimson `C` symbol paired with the spaced uppercase label **CRIMSON HK** or **HK INTELLIGENCE**. The mark should sit within the white sidebar and remain visually aligned with the reference product.

### Signature Brand Color

**Atlas Burgundy — `#7A0019`**. A deeper burgundy than the prior implementation, used sparingly for active states and high-value information.

## Reference-Specific Implementation Rules

The sidebar must be white with a faint right divider. Active items use a pale burgundy background rather than a dark block. The top utility bar and secondary context row use white and warm-grey surfaces. The main title area is compact and does not use a dark hero image. Cards use approximately 4–8 px radii, thin borders, little or no shadow, and consistent internal padding. Section headings and data tables use Source Sans 3. Generated imagery is not used in prominent interface areas because the reference is a utilitarian product UI rather than an image-led experience.

## Placeholder Rules

Incomplete subsections remain visible as planned modules with pale amber backgrounds, thin dashed borders, and direct wording such as **“Placeholder — HK-specific content to be added.”** Placeholders should look deliberately unfinished, not broken.
